## Current product rules to preserve
- Virtual **4W** coins only; no buying, cash redemption or monetary reward; user-approved manual grants/debits with reason and immutable audit, no automatic starting balance. Official Mini4WD monetary event fees, prizes and league results are separate.
- Six color families and **four individually numbered, paint-marked marbles per family = 24** for Season 1. The physical competition uses a six-marble starting grid; numbers and identity remain unique even if two of the same family reach the postseason. Avoid uneven coatings and rotate lanes.
- Four races per matchday; each of 24 appears once per day. 10 matchdays / 40 regular races, top 12 seeded into playoff Race A and Race B, top 3 from each progress to 6-marble championship final = **43 total races**.
- First illustrative event: **2026-10-20 13:00 Asia/Manila = 2026-10-20 05:00 UTC**. Dates are schedule inputs, not hard-coded live server deadlines. Server must reject write requests with a timestamp at/after the lock deadline, regardless of client/browser clock. Host can lock earlier; do not reopen a live market by changing the livestream status.
- Separate markets: champion preseason-only, **up to x8**; race winner, **up to x2**; exact place, **up to x3**. Never present a hard ceiling as a guaranteed starting payout. A single entry into an otherwise empty 60/40 pool only funds x0.4; do not invent an x8 locked promise. Apply 60/40 PER dedicated market, not across one mixed wallet. Quotes are indicative if dynamically pooled; distinguish accepted entry amount/weight from projected and actual return.
- Season 1 champion entries close at first start. Participants may back multiple distinct marbles subject to wallet balance. Earlier preseason entries may receive published 4/2/1 weighting windows; define exact deadlines before activation. Additional tickets change the next indicative quote immediately. Player sees only own wallet/tickets, offered quotes, event/results and standings. Operator privately sees aggregate exposures; never expose totals/stakes via public API, HTML or client bundle.
- Proposed 10% deduction on allowed **preseason cancellation** (100 → 90 returned); record deduction in separate ledger; do not silently reduce still-active ticket stake/weight. An active championship ticket's voluntary cash-out is a separately funded buyout of the ticket, not a refund at face value. No further entry/cancellation/cash-out while a relevant race is locked/live.
- Unclaimed and cap-limited prize balance: **no automatic carryover**; post-season operator/charity disposition must be in published rules and settlement audit. Adjusting pool allocations or revising outcomes to secure the operator result is forbidden.
- Host: save season + two future season configurations, livestream link (YouTube or Facebook), single Lock All Bets action, auto scheduled lock, validated result form, independent confirmation and atomic/idempotent server settlement; scores, payouts and private account screens refresh. Host manual virtual coin adjustment requires audit and cannot modify locked accepted slips. Never conflate simulation sample standings with verified real races.

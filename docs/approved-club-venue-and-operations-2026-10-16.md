# Approved operating decisions — Greenland Mini4WD / Silent Empire
Date: 2026-10-16. Source: owner-approved planning. Documentation is not a permit, engineering signoff or deployed transactional service.

## Membership
- Free account and racer profile persist.
- An optional Club Pass will be separate from legacy earned-day access. A policy retirement of the 20 DKK per day accrual can only be made at a published, prospective effective date after migrating payment confirmation/refunds/history; existing earned time and ticket rights are preserved. Never erase or silently expire accrued benefits.
- Introductory starter-car entitlement, once per eligible customer, is limited to tagged cheapest/common basic Shelf 1 SKUs. Upgraded B-MAX cars are full-price purchases. No unverified inventory, refund/cancellation or paid subscription endpoints may go live.
- Profile/racer photography is opt-in, and public display name separate from legal identity.

## Races and prize distribution
- New regular races created using the updated admin interface must advertise 35% of actual paid ticket revenue as prize pool and 65% retained for club operations. Existing advertised events retain 70/30. Separate special or sponsored championships need event-specific published terms.
- Club retention is **not owner salary or net profit**: it funds payroll, holiday pay, employer levy, utilities, track upkeep, repairs, coffee and supplies. Bonus/free tickets increase attendance but not paid pool. Post the split on the event page, in the event description and on the ticket page.
- Photo/video marketing (FB, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) is not guaranteed monthly revenue; media release workflow and minors' guardian permission are distinct from free registration.

## Filming and CCTV
- The entrance sign should identify the **separately marked event filming zone**, operating schedule and directions to an alternate non-filmed area where practicable. Film the racing area rather than indiscriminately filming entrances, toilets, spectators and unrelated neighbours.
- Publication of identifiable featured closeups, interviews and children requires appropriate lawful permissions. An entrance sign does not waive legal rights.
- Security CCTV is separate: controller name/contact, security-only purpose, viewing permissions, actual limited retention and complaint contact displayed at the site. Do not use security footage for marketing. Do not record ordinary CCTV audio, washrooms or private neighbour areas.
- Before installing or going live, complete a controller-specific privacy notice with actual company details, coverage, contact and retention. See https://da.datatilsynet.gl/regler-og-vejledning/tv-overvaagning and https://da.datatilsynet.gl/borger/sociale-netvaerk-og-internettet .

## Staff, laptop and audit
- Continue the existing TimeLog app as sole employee timekeeping/payroll hours source. Proposed ordinary 115 DKK/h, Sunday +50 DKK/h, paid time from verified time sheets; no duplicate clocking in the Mini4WD app.
- Owner alone may use full remote administration. Employee POS requires managed company laptop enrollment plus individual named session, server-verified staff roles and least-privilege write access; no browser-local flag or IP-only lock. Require step-up owner/supervisor authorisation for refunds, voids, discounts, drawer discrepancies and stock overrides.
- Immutable/restricted audit events: staff ID, station/device ID, time, transaction ID, SKU/quantity/price, payment method, overrides, refunds, stock before/after when adjusted, check-in and daily till close. Do not collect full card credentials.
- DEVICE ENROLMENT / POS TRANSACTIONS are NOT live in this revision. Mini4WD database was not accessible in the current Supabase connector account; neither a secure remote device token nor audit persistence can responsibly be invented.

## Venue scope, subject to planning/building approval
- Owner's intended development: 3 × 40-foot High Cube units at ground level; 2 × 40-foot High Cube units on second floor; optional future third upper unit only if engineering, site/land allocation and budget support it.
- Ground floor: track, defined start/marshal/queue, limited public seating/pit space, sales and refreshments, two accessible/appropriately specified toilets in a serviced wet zone. Upstairs: quieter member pit/assembly plus separately lockable Silent Empire workspace; provide ground-floor equivalent facilities for accessibility. A single interior stair must not be assumed to satisfy fire/egress/accessibility requirements.
- Budget beyond the 5/6 shells: engineered wall openings, foundation/anchoring, stairs, insulation against Nuuk climate, heat/ventilation, electrical service, potable-water supply, wastewater/sewer service, toilets, egress, snow and wind load, toilet and food hygiene approvals as relevant. No build or upper-floor occupancy before land allocation, architect/engineer approval, municipal building permit, utilities and fire assessment.
- Where Silent Empire and Mini4WD are separate operations, target a 50:50 split of eligible **shared** utility bills through written agreement, consistent invoices, and periodic review using meter/usage evidence. Dedicated equipment costs belong to the relevant operator. The agreement should account for which party leases/owns the site and provides public facilities, not merely move deductions without factual basis.
- Neither specific annual plot fee nor utility connection quote has been verified. Seek a site-specific written answer at https://kp.sermersooq.gl/da/ .
